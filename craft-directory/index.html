Pottery & Ceramics

Ramesh Kumar Prajapati ✔ Verified

🎨 Blue Pottery  ·  📍 Rajasthan (North India)  ·  ⏳ 25 years of mastery

About the Artisan

Third-generation Blue Pottery artisan from Jaipur. Learned the craft from his grandfather and has trained over 40 students. Works with a small family workshop preserving traditional Persian-influenced techniques.

Technique & Materials

Quartz stone powder, Fuller earth, natural dyes, hand painting. Unlike other pottery, Blue Pottery does not use clay — instead made from a unique mix of quartz powder and natural gums.

The Craft — Blue Pottery

Distinctive blue-glazed pottery from Jaipur, Rajasthan. Introduced to Jaipur in the 17th century from Persia, it became a signature craft of the region. Each piece takes 15-20 days to complete.

Our Story

About the Craft Directory

A digital bridge between India's master artisans and the world.

Our Mission

The Traditional Craft Directory is a centralized digital platform designed to document, promote, and make discoverable India's traditional artisans and craft communities. We bridge the gap between rural master craftspeople and genuine buyers, researchers, and cultural institutions.

By providing verified, direct-access artisan profiles, we eliminate layers of intermediaries that have historically diminished both artisan incomes and cultural authenticity.

◆

Authenticity

Every profile is manually verified against trusted craft organizations and government sources.

★

Fair Access

Artisans reach markets directly — no intermediary commissions, no lost identity.

✔

Preservation

Digital documentation ensures dying crafts survive for future generations.
